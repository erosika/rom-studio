# Charm Shop sample: brief

A demo-only story, written for this isolated folder, not drawn from your ongoing game.
It uses the supported decompiled GBA engine and existing character sprites.

- **Place.** A cyber charm atelier: chrome counter, pink glass cases of electronic charms,
  a chunky CRT, and a server door. The front exit leads to the older Ribbon Room prototype.
- **You.** A visitor who has just stepped inside. A new game starts in the front room.
- **The person.** The charm keeper, who assembles tiny circuit charms and stands behind the
  counter. Friendly, brief, proud of small work.
- **The look.** Pink acrylic, pearl housings, chrome and cyan screens over a blue-gray floor.
  Map lights stay steady; no animated tile callback or wandering keeper.
- **The first thing that happens.** You talk to the keeper, read the charm case, and walk
  through to the Ribbon Room and back.

Places and their voice: `lore/charm-shop.md`.
